## About

Scenli is an AI-powered interior design platform that allows homeowners, renters, and design enthusiasts to upload photos of their existing spaces and receive photorealistic redesign concepts complete with curated, shoppable product recommendations. By combining advanced generative AI with real-world product sourcing, Scenli eliminates the cost and guesswork of hiring a traditional interior designer. Their platform covers both indoor and outdoor spaces, making them the most accessible end-to-end AI design solution available for everyday consumers.

**What makes us different:** Shoppable product plans tied to photorealistic renders with architecture preservation — competitors offer one or two of these, not all three.
